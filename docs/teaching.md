# Teaching

## PM520 — Advanced Statistical Computing

Lectures and programming labs connect the theory of statistical inference with its implementation in Python and JAX. Students learn to solve linear systems accurately, use automatic differentiation for gradient-based optimization, and apply variational inference to large datasets.

The course is designed for biostatistics graduate students in their second year or beyond and for graduate students in other fields who want to develop computational methods for their research.

[Browse the PM520 lab notebooks and homework on GitHub](https://github.com/USCbiostats/PM520)

## PM570 — Statistical Methods in Human Genetics

This course introduces statistical methods for analyzing human genetic data. Students run genome-wide association studies in Python notebooks and use statistical theory to understand how population structure can affect their results. They also map associations between genetic variants and molecular traits.

[Browse the PM570 lecture notebooks on GitHub](https://github.com/USCbiostats/PM570-Colab)
